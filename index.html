Shah Rukh Khan

Explore Films ↗ The Legacy ↘

From television screens to becoming the face of Indian cinema globally.

Shah Rukh Khan, often referred to as the "Baadshah of Bollywood," is an Indian actor, film producer, and television personality. With a career spanning over three decades, he has starred in more than 90 films and earned numerous accolades, including 14 Filmfare Awards.

Known for his unmatched versatility—effortlessly transitioning between iconic romance, intense drama, sharp comedy, and high-octane action—he isn't just an actor; he is an emotion for millions worldwide. His journey is a testament to dedication, charm, and the sheer power of dreams.
